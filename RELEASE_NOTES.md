151 common Kenyan birds, one Picture → Name card each, plus a Look-alikes subdeck.

**New in v1.6:** the Grosbeak weaver (tagged `batch_003`). Its photo comes from iNaturalist under CC BY-NC 4.0, so it may not be used commercially; see the README.

**New in v1.5:** two new Look-alikes groups for weavers. One covers the yellow masked weavers (Village, Speke's and Baglafecht), the other the brown weavers (White-browed sparrow-weaver and Grey-capped social weaver). That makes 5 more cards.

**New in v1.4:** revealing the answer on a Look-alikes card no longer scrolls the photo off the top of the screen.

**New in v1.3:** a **Birds of Kenya::Look-alikes** subdeck with 36 cards in 15 groups of similar birds: flamingos, collared doves, hornbills, starlings, sparrows, fiscals, robin-chats, four sunbird pairs, two raptor pairs, vultures and lapwings. Each card shows a photo and asks which bird in the group it is. The back gives the answer, a short field mark and photos of the other birds in the group. The cards use their own photos, different from the main deck's, and the photo changes from day to day. The subdeck has its own preset (5 new cards a day); start it once the main cards for those birds are familiar.

**New in v1.2:** photo credits no longer appear on the back of each card. They are listed in [CREDITS.md](https://github.com/alxmjo/birds-kenya/blob/main/CREDITS.md), linked from the deck description.

**New in v1.1:** 50 more species (tagged `batch_002`), including waterbirds, raptors, hornbills, sunbirds and starlings.

**To install:** download `Birds_of_Kenya.apkg` below and open it with Anki (File → Import on desktop). On first import, tick *Import any deck presets*.

**To update from an earlier version:** import over it and choose *Update notes: If newer*. Tick *Import any deck presets* too, so the Look-alikes subdeck gets its settings. If you already have v1.3, also choose *Update note types: If newer*, so the card layout change comes through. Your review history is kept.
